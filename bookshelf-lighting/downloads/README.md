## Electrical design

One Akyga AKY0627 / LP675568 protected 1S 3500 mAh battery. Battery is 68 × 55 × 6.7 mm, nominal 3.7 V. Manufacturer confirms overcharge, overdischarge and short-circuit PCM, 150 mm lead and mating JST connector/socket. Supplier Ropla lists maximum charge/discharge 0.5C = 1.75 A; use this stricter limit, even though MrSmart lists 1C. TP4056 charger is at most 1 A. No parallel battery packs.

Battery + → F1 1.6 A time-lag fuse → common of a three-terminal ON-OFF-ON switch. Map terminals by continuity before applying power. CHARGE connects common to TP4056 B+. LIGHT connects common to Elkim boost BAT+. OFF disconnects both. Battery − connects to TP4056 B− and boost BAT−. TP4056 OUT+/OUT− are unused: the pack's own PCM protects the pack in both operating modes. Never depend on the unused TP4056 OUT protection as protection for this wiring.

A complete USB-A to USB-C cable plugs into TP4056. Use a regulated 5 V USB-A adapter, at least 1 A. Boost USB-C/IN pads MUST remain unconnected; its socket faces inward and is not a charging port in this design. The mode switch prevents lamp operation during charging and prevents charger-board standby drain during lighting/storage. Never connect the spare battery in parallel.

Boost VOUT+ → F2 250 mA time-lag fuse → positive trunk. Boost VOUT− → negative trunk. Each of five parallel lamp branches has its own series 1 kΩ 0.25 W resistor on the positive side. Start with 1 kΩ; retain existing lamp electronics. Do not exceed measured original lamp current. Increase resistance if necessary; do not lower it blindly to gain brightness. Remove ALL original cells before external supply. A shorted lamp after 1 kΩ can draw at most 4.5 mA at 4.5 V; all five together at most 22.5 mA, excluding faults bypassing resistors.

The basic harness is soldered directly. Ten detachable connector pairs are an optional 990 kr workshop purchase, not needed for the basic design. Up to 5 m trunk + five 0.5 m branches = 15 conductor-metres. Mark the positive conductor at every end. Each splice is separately heat-shrunk, with positive/negative splices staggered inside the T cover.

## Box dimensions

Controller shell: 180 × 112 × 34.4 mm assembled (switch lever adds approx. 11 mm above the lid), base height 32 mm, lid plate 2.4 mm. T enclosure: 48 × 22 × 11.8 mm assembled. Battery guard: 88 × 64 × 2.4 mm. Fit coupon: 72 × 28 × 9.4 mm.

Battery pocket: 72 × 57 mm. Pack reference: 68 × 55 × 6.7 mm on a platform at Z=4.4. Guard underside Z=15, giving 3.9 mm free space above the specified pack; no compression. Four guard screws sit outside the pouch area. No straps over the battery. Do not force a swollen or differently sized pack into the tray.

Reserved envelopes (these are intentionally larger than known boards, not exact models): charger 34 × 22 × 12 mm, boost 38 × 26 × 12 mm, USB plug 24 × 14 × 12 mm, each fuse holder 60 × 18 × 18 mm, switch and terminal wiring 20 × 20 × 24 mm. Elkim publishes approx. 33 × 24 × 4 mm for boost. Charger PCB and fuse-holder exterior dimensions are not published by these stores; physically compare them against these envelopes before printing the complete controller. This is not a claim of measured fit.

Two 4 × 4 mm open cable seams allow whole USB connectors to stay intact. The selected cable is specified Ø3.5 mm; the twisted 2 × Ø1.1 mm lamp wire also fits within a 4 mm slot. Cable ties provide internal strain relief; the case opening itself is not a cable clamp. Pull-test the completed relief and adjust using existing heat-shrink if needed. No weather sealing is claimed.

Four adjustable PCB rails (44 × 14 × 6 mm) sit on posts at Z=9 and put the PCB underside at Z=15. Each rail is secured by two M3 × 8 screws through 12 × 3.4 mm slots (8.6 mm travel). Adjust to the actual clear PCB edges before tightening; screw heads remain below the board plane. This accommodates the selected boards without assuming their width from a loose enclosure envelope. Place only bare PCB edges on rails. Tie across unused edges, avoiding components; do not install the boards loose. Reposition ties or edit the rail spacing if the supplied board revision differs. Fuse holders are restrained in their two reserved bays using four ties.

## Assembly and commissioning

1. Inspect one actual lamp before changing it. Measure original current in series with fresh cells, never across the battery stack. Identify +/− battery input pads and preserve the original electronics and switch. The exact lamp-contact connection is still unknown. If there are only unsolderable spring contacts, stop at the lamp: a contact adapter must be designed from its measurements. Do not damage the lamp by heating steel springs or forcing a wire through the cover.
2. Measure received controller parts against reserved envelopes. Try cable, switch and screws in coupon. Make all harness wiring with battery and USB disconnected. Solder only the mating connector and module pads, never battery pouch/cell terminals. Insulate every terminal, including switch lugs and battery connector.
3. Measure all switch states with continuity, verify no +/− short, install the correct separate fuses, then connect one protected pack. Do not short the pack to test the PCM or fuses.
4. Select CHARGE; connect USB to TP4056. Verify charging voltage reaches no more than 4.2 V within the board's specified tolerance and current is around/under its 1 A rating (pack limit 1.75 A). First charge on a nonflammable surface, away from books, under observation. Use the listed thermometer (battery included), with the insulated sensor held against the pouch without compression, and also check the case near the charger through a whole cycle. Stop at 40 °C or abnormal temperature rise; the pack charging limit is 45 °C. Do not bridge solder pads with a metal probe. This is a commissioning screen, not calibrated thermal qualification. A charger IC's thermal regulation is not proof that the printed case is cool enough. Do not install in books until thermal behaviour is checked.
5. Disconnect USB. Select LIGHT and set VOUT to 4.5 V before lamps. Also check with a spare 1 kΩ resistor as a light test load. The supply can otherwise reach 27 V when incorrectly adjusted. Measure polarity at the farthest T cover.
6. Remove the lantern's original cells and connect one 1 kΩ branch. Compare current against the original reading. Check brightness, stability, contact reliability and warmth. Increase resistance or inspect electronics if necessary. Do not expand to all five until the first branch behaves correctly.
7. Check all five, measure total battery-side operating current, then conduct a long lighting session. Confirm wire restraint, no pinched pouch, lid fit without force and charging termination. Take box into free air for charging. OFF for storage.

Runtime example only: battery 12.95 Wh × illustrative 80% boost efficiency = 10.36 Wh before idle losses. Five 1 mA lamps at 4.5 V → about 460 h; five 3 mA lamps → about 153 h. These are not measured results; undocumented boost idle current can shorten runtime considerably. Estimate more directly from measured battery current: 3500 mAh / battery mA. Charge: ideal minimum 3.5 h at 1 A; allow roughly 5–7 h for CV/thermal limiting, then measure the first full cycle.
