## Electrical status

No complete assembly schematic for the revised controller is approved. The Elkim board is only a candidate. Battery charging current, overdischarge protection, battery-side wiring protection, holder length, idle draw and temperature still need resolution. Concurrent charging and lamp operation is not a documented feature.

Five lanterns use a shared black two-wire trunk, with separate parallel branches and detachable contacts. Remove all original cells before external power. Inspect one actual lantern and measure its original current in series before modifying the other four. A 1 kohm branch resistor is a conservative starting point for a later test at approximately 4.5 V; the final resistance depends on the lamp electronics. Do not connect a current-mode meter directly across a battery. No runtime or physical fit is claimed.
