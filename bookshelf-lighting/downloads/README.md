# Bookshelf lantern power system — prototype v1

Designed 20 October 2026 for five GlobalVibeArt lanterns and up to 5 m of shared trunk, with up to 0.5 m drop to each lamp. The seller specifies three AG3/LR41 cells. The actual lamp internals, required current and battery-contact arrangement have not been inspected.

**Delivered:** editable CadQuery source, independent STL and STEP parts, reference assembly, CAD preview, shopping list and assembly instructions. Geometry and meshes are checked. **Pending:** actual component fit, lamp modification, electrical/thermal tests, slicing and printing. These are prototype files, not a tested finished electrical product.

## Wiring layout

```text
External USB power bank -- USB cable -- [CONTROL BOX] == paired black trunk == T == T == T == T == T
                                                                          |    |    |    |    |
                                                                      unplugging connectors
                                                                          |    |    |    |    |
                                                                        lamps, in parallel
```

Only one two-wire pair leaves the control box. At each location, a short lamp lead branches from the trunk. A small printed cover hides the soldered T and branch resistor. The trunk continues independently of the removable lamp: unplugging a lamp does not disconnect downstream lamps. The last T has its outgoing trunk slot left unused; insulate and retain the trunk ends inside that cover. It may be extended later.

## Box dimensions and component fit

- Assembled control box: **110 × 58 × 38.4 mm**, excluding rocker protrusion and cables.
- Walls and floor: 2.4 mm. Removable lid: 2.4 mm plus a 1.6 mm locating lip inside the box.
- Switch opening: 20.2 mm with anti-rotation notch; selected switch specifies nominal 20 mm hole and panel thickness 1.0–2.5 mm. Published 23 mm flange and conservative 23.2 mm below-lid envelope were used. CAD switch-to-floor clearance: 10.4 mm, before soldered wires.
- USB inlet: 5.5 mm circular opening; trunk outlet: 4 mm opening at the opposite end. **These are cable exits, not connector sockets or certified cable glands.** Thread the loose cable ends before soldering. Hole fit and cable-tie pull retention require physical testing; enlarge or reduce the parameters if the actual USB cable diameter differs.
- Regulator supports accommodate its approximately 17 × 11.4 mm board. Two separate tie bridges allow padded retention without pretending the board has mounting screw holes. Leave the adjustment pot accessible and prevent ties from pressing on components. Fit the input capacitor beside VIN and the insulated output fuse on the output side.
- Power bank remains outside, so changing bank model does not require a new enclosure.
- T cover: **48 × 22 × 11.8 mm**, no terminal blocks. Soldered continuous trunk + resistor branch. Two screw closures and adjustable tie bridges. The short lamp lead should be additionally tied to the trunk inside so the side joint carries no pull.

## Electrical connections

```text
USB red +5 V ---- SPST switch ---- VIN   Pololu S7V8A   VOUT ---- F1 ---- trunk POSITIVE
                                  |                    (~4.5 V)
                               C1 100 µF
                                  |
USB black GND ------------------- GND -------------------------- trunk NEGATIVE

At EACH tap:
trunk POSITIVE ------ resistor Rn ------ connector ------ lamp pack-positive contact
trunk NEGATIVE ------------------------ connector ------ lamp pack-negative contact
```

C1 positive goes to switched VIN, its marked negative to GND. S7V8A SHDN may be left disconnected as documented by Pololu. The switch disconnects the regulator input. All negative wires share GND. Do not connect VOUT back to USB red. F1 is on the positive OUTPUT, not a resistor shared by all lamps.

**First-lamp test is required before modifying the remaining lamps.** Remove every cell. Inspect whether the lamp has a bare LED, resistor or electronics, and identify the overall battery-stack endpoints. Do not assume a contact's polarity from its shape, and never attach replacement power with batteries still installed. Keep the original switch and board unless inspection supports a different connection.

Measure the original lamp current with fresh batteries using the meter IN SERIES. Never put a current-mode meter directly across a battery or supply. With the lamp disconnected, set regulator output near 4.5 V and check polarity with a voltage-mode meter. Connect one lamp initially through 1 kΩ. At 4.5 V that resistor alone caps branch current below 4.5 mA (approximately 4.8 mA allowing 5% tolerance), although actual light level depends on the lamp. It may initially be dim. Record lamp current and brightness; choose the final resistor after understanding the circuit, keeping current within the original/rated LED limit. Do not bypass the resistor merely because the lamp is dim. If there is an onboard controller that cannot run through the resistor, resolve its power input requirements before continuing.

The resistor assortment allows tuning without selecting an unverified final resistor now. A regulated supply can provide more current than small button cells even at the same nominal voltage. [Adafruit explains the current-limiting requirement](https://learn.adafruit.com/lets-put-leds-in-things/basic-recipe).

## Harness assembly

1. Lay out the actual lamp locations. Cut two equal 5 m trunk wires (or actual required length). Keep enough slack to move books without pulling lamps.
2. Mark the positive wire at both ends and every planned tap using a small label or a permanent mark hidden inside each cover. Both wires are black. Verify with continuity mode before power.
3. Lightly twist the pair so it behaves as one cable; hold it at the back edge of shelves. Short same-colour loops hold it discreetly to existing supports. Optional adhesive clips can be added after measuring shelf finish and cable fit.
4. At each location, leave the trunk intact and strip only a small section from each conductor. Stagger the positive and negative joints. Preload heat-shrink before making each joint. Solder a branch to each conductor, with that lamp's resistor on the positive branch. Individually insulate; the two bare joints must never touch. Do not put a resistor in the onward trunk.
5. Fit the joints into a T cover. Tie down the incoming and outgoing pair and lash the branch to it for strain relief. Attach the lamp-side connector pigtail. Keep the connector behind the books and extend with black wire to the lantern. Continuity-check its polarity before soldering at the lamp.
6. Thread the USB and trunk cables through the box before soldering. Secure each to its printed bridge with a cable tie, snug enough to hold without cutting insulation. Mount regulator above floor on supports; insulate and restrain its leads, capacitor and fuse. Route switch wires with a service loop so the lid lifts without pulling pads.
7. Test with one lamp, then all five. At 100 mA total and a 5 m 0.14 mm² copper trunk, a conservative end-loaded copper voltage-drop estimate is 0.125 V before fuse/joints; distributed loads are better. Actual v1 current is expected to be much lower but remains unmeasured. Longer/more powerful installations need the wire and fuse revisited.
8. Run the assembled system on the bench with lid installed, monitoring temperatures, output stability and low-load power-bank operation. Check cable pull retention and switching before placing it among books. Keep the power bank accessible for charging and inspection.

## Print and fit

Print one `fit-coupon.stl` first. It contains the selected switch opening, USB cable gauge holes of 4.5 / 5 / 5.5 mm and screw pilot towers of 2.5 / 2.6 mm. Verify switch latching and USB cable fit; test only about 5 mm of screw engagement in the towers. This coupon does not qualify the complete assembly.

Then print one controller base and lid, and five T bases and lids. Every STL is in millimetres. Main lid is exported **flat outside face down, locating lip up**; do not turn it lip-down and bridge across the whole lid. Base prints bottom down. T covers print flat. Suggested starting profile: 0.20 mm layers, four walls, five top/bottom layers, 15–20% infill, supports off. Tiny tie tunnels bridge a few millimetres; inspect them in slicer and clear with a tie. Use the printer/material's correct temperature profile. There is no sliced printer file or verified print-time estimate in this package.

Screws form threads in plastic; start straight and tighten gently. Repeated service may eventually need inserts or a revised lid. No print or fit has been tested yet.

## Files and validation

`print-files/` contains five distinct geometries in both STL and STEP. Only the T pair is printed five times. `assembly-reference.step` includes component envelopes for reference; do not print it as a single object. `design.py` contains editable dimensions and regeneration. `render.py` makes the CAD preview from exported solids. `validation.json` records validity, connected solids, closed mesh checks, sizes and lid/switch clearances.

Regenerate with:

```sh
.venv/bin/python design.py
.venv/bin/python render.py
```

The local Python environment is not included in the download. To recreate it, use Python with `cadquery`, `trimesh`, `numpy`, `matplotlib` and `vtk`; versions are in `requirements.txt`.
