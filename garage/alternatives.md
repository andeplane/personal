# Garage storage: build the walls, choose the rear separately

## Principles

1. Use the recess depth behind the studs before taking passage space. Retain the existing ledges, diagonal braces and wired charger.
2. Build useful, aligned shelves: modest projection low down, more depth high up. Preserve the loft and its approach.
3. Choose wall storage independently of rear storage. A deeper rear rack can shorten the left run at the corner, but must not silently change the selected wall system.
4. Leave adaptable space for changing belongings. One sled and the folded ladder get places; backpacks do not dictate the layout. No owned-box count is assumed.
5. Prefer ready-sized products. Where cutting substantially improves use of the wall, identify the real stock board, supports, cuts and whole-board cost.
