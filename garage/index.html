Use the space that is already here.

  1. Recesses first. Use the depth behind the studs before taking space from the passage.
  2. More depth high up, where it works. Long side shelves for flat/light items under the loft; taller storage at the rear; bulky seasonal items on the existing loft.
  3. Keep the garage usable. Preserve charger access, a removable sled, the folded ladder and a route to the loft. Show the loss of clearance with the car parked.
  4. Flexible capacity. Mix small-item shelves, larger openings and an open floor bay. Bags are examples, not a fixed design brief.
  5. Buyable parts. Prefer uncut stock sizes. Show the exact stock board, cut and whole-board price wherever fitting is worthwhile.

The 2.10 m loft underside is still an assumption. A 1.95 m side shelf leaves only about 15 cm for contents and its brackets extend down to about 1.73 m. Door tracks, actual head clearance and structural fixings must be checked before installation.
