THE PLAN I’D BUILD TOWARDS

Three layers. One garden.

Keep the Hue Bridge, Home Assistant Green and Sonos you already have.

01 / THE INFESTATION

Make the wall crawl.

Start with one 30 cm and one 45 cm latex spider. If the samples look good, build up to 20–30 mixed sizes, with textile webs and one oversized focal spider.

02 / THE CEMETERY

Give darkness some depth.

A few substantial graves, hidden Hue Lily spots and a purple wall wash. Add ground fog only where wind and a protected power position allow it.

03 / THE REVEAL

Something moves.

Test projected tarantulas on the wall first. Then prototype one falling spider or one moving grave, with a deliberate cue in our existing show.

A BETTER VERSION OF THE SOLAR STRINGS

One battery. Original adapters.
Proper control.

Use good low-power LED strings and retain their original adapters. A larger power station can supply them through a controllable plug; the battery stays dry inside the dukkestue. Use mains power where available for longer runtime.
